# ISS-790 — [Color] Check Vibrant color pallette

State: CLOSED, stateReason COMPLETED · opened 2026-09-20 by ladybug-me · closed 2026-09-20.

## Problem (original report, condensed)

Filed by the maintainer as a check on the vibrant palette (the issue body is empty; the title is the report): does the `vibrant` color variant still look as strong as before the color-engine change?

(Context from the linked threads: this grew out of #763, where a user noticed the `vibrant` variant looked less "strong" on the new color engine and asked whether the old engine could be kept; the maintainer's replies here are the answer.)

## Resolution

What maintainers/collaborators actually said (verbatim):

> **ladybug-me** (OWNER, 2026-09-20):
> @MITSIKU, after analysis, I found that the older color engine (`materialyoucolor` pip package) used 2021 Material Design 3.
> The latest engine (`matugen`) uses Google's official 2024+ Material You Utilities.
>
> Which means balanced tonal elevation.

> **ladybug-me** (OWNER, 2026-09-20):
> You can try these display options, they tend to boost colors for me.
>
> (a screenshot of the display options was attached; image content is not quoted)

The issue was closed by ladybug-me within the same second as the first comment (closed_at 2026-09-20T13:57:08Z); the follow-up suggestion came two minutes later.

Corpus note: the new `matugen` engine intentionally produces more balanced (less saturated) tonal elevation than the old `materialyoucolor` engine — it is not a bug. A color-intensity control for exactly this ask was added via #791 ("Add color intensity slider", 0xSolanaceae: "added"), and the display options screenshot in this thread shows the maintainer's own boost settings.
