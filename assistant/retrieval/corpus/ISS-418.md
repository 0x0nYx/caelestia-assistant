## Resolution

What maintainers/collaborators actually said (verbatim, lightly trimmed):

> **ladybug-me** (OWNER, 2026-08-05):
> As you can see the workspace switcher in bar is not showing per workspace opened window icons.
> Its a clear indicator that kwinactivewindowbridge has crashed.
> Happens when a dialogue saying `Quickshell has crashed` pops up.
> I already pushed a fix for it in dev yesterday.
> Simply restarting the shell will fix this.

> **ladybug-me** (OWNER, 2026-08-05):
> killall quickshell
> killall qs
> caelestia shell -d

> **ladybug-me** (OWNER, 2026-08-05):
> Yes this happens when quickshell itself crashes.
> I am still trying to find out its root cause, whether its us or quickshell.
>
> Since you have been installing/updating the shell many times earlier, I recommend doing a clean reset of the shell.
>
> >! This will reset nexus to defaults
> ```bash
> rm -rf ~/.config/caelestia
> rm -rf ~/.config/quickshell
> rm -rf ~/.local/usr/lib/qt6/qml/Caelestia
> ```
>
> Then reinstall using setup.sh

> **ladybug-me** (OWNER, 2026-08-05):
> fixed
